Know About Us

Max Auto Mobile – Professional Vehicle Service & Spare Parts

Max Auto Mobile is a reliable automobile service center based in Wawulagala, Baddegama, Sri Lanka. Established on October 15, 2015, by P. D. Upul Sanjeewa, we specialize in professional vehicle servicing and genuine spare parts sales for a wide range of vehicles.

With experienced technicians and modern equipment, we deliver trusted maintenance, repairs, and quality auto parts to keep your vehicle performing at its best. Max Auto Mobile is committed to honest service, quality workmanship, and customer satisfaction.

Your trusted auto service partner in Baddegama.
